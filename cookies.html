Overview

What This Page Covers

This Cookie Policy explains how Minecraft Club of America ("MCA," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar browser storage (such as localStorage) on minecraftclubofamerica.me (the "Site"). It supplements our Privacy Policy, which covers how we handle personal information more broadly.

Summary

The Short Version

We only use browser storage that's strictly necessary to make the Site work — to keep you signed in and to remember your light/dark theme choice. We do not use cookies or browser storage for advertising, cross-site tracking, or analytics, and we don't load any third-party advertising or analytics scripts. Because we don't use any non-essential cookies, there's nothing here that requires your opt-in consent — but we still want you to know exactly what's stored and why.

What We Use

Storage Categories

Name / type Purpose Category Duration
Supabase auth session (localStorage) Keeps you signed in between visits Strictly necessary Until you sign out, or the session expires
mca_theme (localStorage) Remembers your light/dark mode preference Strictly necessary / functional Until you clear browser data
mca_cart_<user id> (localStorage) Remembers items in your Shop cart Strictly necessary / functional Until you check out, clear it, or clear browser data
Cloudflare (hosting/CDN) Security and bot mitigation (Turnstile) during signup Strictly necessary Session-based; set by Cloudflare, not by MCA

None of this is a cookie in the traditional sense (a value automatically sent with every request) except the Cloudflare security check — everything else is localStorage, which stays on your device and is only read by our own code when you visit the Site.

Not Used

What We Don't Use

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, Meta/Facebook Pixel, or similar). We don't sell or share browsing data with data brokers or ad networks. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Your Choices

Managing Storage

Because everything we store is strictly necessary, blocking it will simply sign you out and reset your theme/cart preferences rather than change what data we collect. You can clear cookies and site data at any time through your browser's settings, or use a private/incognito window to avoid persistent storage entirely. Signing out from your account page also clears your session.

Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if what we store changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
