Commitment

Our Commitment

Minecraft Club of America ("MCA," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to making minecraftclubofamerica.me usable by everyone, including members with disabilities. MCA serves students from elementary school through high school, so accessibility matters to a wide range of ages and abilities, and we treat it as an ongoing responsibility rather than a one-time project.

Standard

Conformance Target

We work toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard most commonly referenced by U.S. accessibility law and guidance, including Title II/III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) as applied to websites and Section 508. We can't promise every page meets every success criterion at all times, but this is the bar we build and review against, including things like keyboard operability, semantic structure and ARIA roles, color contrast, alt text on images, and accessible form controls.

Known limitations

Known Limitations

Some areas of the Site — particularly staff-only admin tools — have not been audited as thoroughly as member-facing pages, since they're used by a smaller group and aren't part of the public experience. If you encounter a barrier anywhere on the Site, we want to know about it (see "Feedback" below) so we can fix it.

Feedback

Feedback & Contact

If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of minecraftclubofamerica.me, please let us know — email us at minecraftclubofamerica@gmail.com or reach out on Discord, and describe the page and the problem you ran into. We'll do our best to respond promptly and provide the information or functionality you need through an alternative method in the meantime.

Updates

Changes to This Statement

We may update this statement as our accessibility work continues. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
