Overview

Who We Are

Minecraft Club of America ("MCA," "we," "us," or "our") operates minecraftclubofamerica.me (the "Site") and its associated Discord community and Minecraft server (together, the "Services"). MCA is a club for students from elementary school through middle/junior high and high school. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Services, how we use it, and the choices you and, where applicable, your parent or guardian have.

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

Collection

Information We Collect

Account information. You may create an account by signing in with Discord or Google, or with an email and password. When you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive basic profile information from that provider — typically your name, email address, and profile picture — which is used solely to create and authenticate your account. We do not receive your Google or Discord password.

Date of birth. We ask everyone for their date of birth when they create an account (or, for Discord/Google sign-in, immediately afterward, since those providers don't let us collect it during sign-in itself). This determines how your account is treated under this policy — see "Children's Privacy" below — and is never asked in a way that suggests a "correct" answer, and can't be changed once submitted.

Profile data. Once your account is active, we store a profile record which may include your display name, username, email address, your role/permissions within the club, and any persistent identifiers from a linked Discord or Google account or verified Minecraft account (a Discord/Google account ID, or your Minecraft username and UUID).

Content you submit. If you contribute to the Site — for example, archive documents, nation submissions, or news posts — we store that content along with your author information.

Automatically collected data. Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers (see below) automatically log technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, for security and reliability purposes.

Cookies and local storage. We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences (such as light/dark theme). We don't use these for advertising.

Google Sign-In

Use of Google User Data

When you choose "Continue with Google," we request only your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile photo) through Google's OAuth sign-in. This information is used exclusively to create your MCA account, verify your identity, and personalize your experience on the Site. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell or transfer Google user data to third parties, except as necessary to provide the Services (see "How We Share Information" below) or as required by law.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purpose

How We Use Information

  • To create, secure, and maintain your account
  • To operate club features — nations, the archive, news, and community tools
  • To communicate with you about the club, moderation, or your submissions
  • To enforce our rules and Terms of Service, and to keep the Services safe
  • To improve and troubleshoot the Site
Sharing

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that operate the Services on our behalf, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores account and profile data
  • Discord and Google — used only if you choose to sign in with, or link, those providers (blocked entirely for under-13 accounts unless a parent has given separate, explicit consent for third-party disclosure — see "Children's Privacy")
  • Cloudflare — our hosting and content-delivery provider
  • Resend — our transactional email provider, used to send account verification codes, password resets, parental-consent notices, and (only if you've opted in) news updates

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of MCA, our members, or the public.

We do not operate any targeted-advertising system, do not sell or share personal information with data brokers or advertisers, and do not collect geolocation data.

Retention

Data Retention

We keep account and profile information for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the Services, and no longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for. Specifically:

  • If you (or, for a child's account, a parent or guardian) request account deletion, the account is deactivated immediately and its data is permanently deleted 7 days later, unless the deletion is cancelled by signing back in during that window.
  • If a parent or guardian declines or later revokes consent for a child's account, the same 7-day deletion applies automatically — there is no separate opt-in required to trigger it.
  • One-time codes and links (sign-in verification, password reset, the parental-consent response link) expire and are purged automatically, typically within minutes to a couple of weeks depending on the type.

Children

Children's Privacy

MCA serves members of all ages, including children under 13, so this section explains how we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) as amended, effective June 23, 2025.

Age screening. Everyone is asked for their date of birth when creating an account, or immediately afterward for Discord/Google sign-in. Nothing about how the question is asked suggests a "correct" answer, and it can't be resubmitted with a different answer once given.

Restricted state before consent. An account that says it belongs to someone under 13 is placed in a restricted state immediately — before it collects anything beyond the date of birth and, once provided, a parent or guardian's email address. A restricted account cannot publish content, set a public display name, appear in the member directory, hold any role, upload files, or have its information disclosed to any third party. This is enforced by the database itself (row-level security and triggers), not only by what the website's interface shows.

Parental consent. We email the parent or guardian a direct notice — separate from this Privacy Policy — explaining that their child provided this contact information, exactly what categories of information we'd collect and how we'd use them, that we need their consent before collecting anything further, that they may review, delete, or refuse further collection at any time, and a link to this full Privacy Policy. The account stays restricted until they respond. This is our "email plus" verifiable parental consent method: a parent's decision through a unique link tied to their email, followed by a permanent portal link they can use afterward at any time. We currently offer only this method, so we do not disclose a child's information to any third party (including linking a Discord or Google account) — that would require a stronger consent method we haven't implemented, and the database blocks it outright until we do.

What we collect from children under 13, once approved. The same limited account information described above (display name, username, email address, and role/permissions) — never more than what activation requires — plus anything the child chooses to submit through club features. We do not use this information for advertising and do not sell it. A verified under-13 account is also, by default, kept out of the public member directory.

Parental rights and the parent portal. The consent email includes a permanent, no-account-required parent portal link. At any time, a parent or guardian can use it to review exactly what's been collected about their child, revoke consent, or request deletion — without needing to contact us first, though you're always welcome to. Revoking consent or requesting deletion immediately returns the account to its restricted state and queues the collected information for deletion on the same 7-day schedule described above.

If you believe a child under 13 has an active, unrestricted account, or has provided us with personal information without this consent process having been completed, please contact us and we will investigate and delete the information as appropriate.

Teens

13-17 Year Olds

Members age 13 through 17 are outside COPPA's scope but are still given privacy-protective defaults, consistent with state minor-privacy laws: no targeted advertising, no sale or sharing of personal information, no public geolocation (we don't collect geolocation from anyone), and a default-off member-directory listing that you can choose to turn on from your account page. These defaults are set automatically based on the date of birth provided at signup.
