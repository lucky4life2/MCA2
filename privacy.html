Overview

Who We Are

Minecraft Club of America ("MCA," "we," "us," or "our") operates minecraftclubofamerica.me (the "Site") and its associated Discord community and Minecraft server (together, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

Collection

Information We Collect

Account information. You may create an account by signing in with Discord or Google, or with an email and password. When you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive basic profile information from that provider — typically your name, email address, and profile picture — which is used solely to create and authenticate your account. We do not receive your Google or Discord password.

Profile data. We store a profile record for your account, which may include your display name, username, email address, and your role/permissions within the club.

Content you submit. If you contribute to the Site — for example, archive documents, nation submissions, or news posts — we store that content along with your author information.

Automatically collected data. Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers (see below) automatically log technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, for security and reliability purposes.

Cookies and local storage. We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences (such as light/dark theme). We don't use these for advertising.

Google Sign-In

Use of Google User Data

When you choose "Continue with Google," we request only your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile photo) through Google's OAuth sign-in. This information is used exclusively to create your MCA account, verify your identity, and personalize your experience on the Site. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell or transfer Google user data to third parties, except as necessary to provide the Services (see "How We Share Information" below) or as required by law.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purpose

How We Use Information

  • To create, secure, and maintain your account
  • To operate club features — nations, the archive, news, and community tools
  • To communicate with you about the club, moderation, or your submissions
  • To enforce our rules and Terms of Service, and to keep the Services safe
  • To improve and troubleshoot the Site
Sharing

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that operate the Services on our behalf, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores account and profile data
  • Discord and Google — used only if you choose to sign in with those providers
  • Cloudflare — our hosting and content-delivery provider

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of MCA, our members, or the public.

Retention

Data Retention

We keep account and profile information for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the Services. If you delete your account or request removal, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable time, except where we're required to retain it for legal or security reasons.

Your Choices

Your Rights

You can review or update your profile information at any time from your account page. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, or to revoke a connected Google/Discord sign-in, contact us using the information below. You can also revoke MCA's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions page.

Children

Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
