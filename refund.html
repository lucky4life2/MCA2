Overview

Who This Applies To

This policy covers purchases made through the MCA Shop at minecraftclubofamerica.me/shop.html, including the annual membership subscription and any physical or digital merchandise. MCA is a volunteer-run, non-commercial club — payments go toward covering server hosting and club operating costs, not profit.

Membership

Membership Subscription

  • Cancel anytime. You can cancel your membership at any time to stop future renewal charges. Cancelling does not refund the current billing period, but you'll keep access through the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Accidental or duplicate charges, and requests made within 7 days of first signing up (and before you've used member-only benefits such as the Minecraft server) are eligible for a full refund.
  • Outside that window, membership fees already charged are generally non-refundable, since they fund server costs for the period already billed — but if something went wrong on our end (a billing error, a duplicate subscription, or a service outage), contact us and we'll make it right.
  • To cancel or request a refund, use the Cancel Membership option on your account page, which sends a request directly to our staff, or contact us using the information below.
Merchandise

Shop Merchandise

  • If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was described, contact us within 14 days of delivery for a full refund or replacement.
  • Because merchandise runs are limited, we generally don't offer refunds for a simple change of mind once an order has shipped — but reach out and we'll see what we can do.
  • Digital items (if any) are refundable only if they were not delivered or are materially defective.
How To Request

Requesting a Refund or Cancellation

The fastest way to cancel a membership or request a refund is through your account page — no need to figure out who to email. You can also reach us directly at minecraftclubofamerica@gmail.com or on Discord. We aim to respond to every request within a few business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method and may take several business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. This policy doesn't limit any refund or cancellation right you have under applicable law.
